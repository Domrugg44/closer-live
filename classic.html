Every prediction is written to an append-only log with the time it was logged, the price available then (when recorded), and its source. Each entry carries a fingerprint chained to the one before it, so any later edit, deletion or reordering breaks the chain; GitHub re-checks it on every change and your browser re-checks it on every visit. Results are filled in automatically from final scores (every 30 minutes). Units and ROI count only picks whose odds were recorded at the time, at 1 unit per pick. Closing line value compares our number with the last line seen before the game.
